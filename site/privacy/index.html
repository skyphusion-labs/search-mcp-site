Privacy

This site does not track you

This page lists what the search-mcp website does and does not do with your visit.

This website

  • No cookies. The site sets none.
  • No trackers and no analytics. There is no analytics script and no tracking pixel.
  • Nothing from anyone else. Every script, stylesheet, image and font comes from this site. There are no third-party fonts, embeds, frames or CDNs. The site's Content Security Policy enforces this in your browser, and its CI fails any change that adds one.
  • No search calls. The site does not embed the ask widget and does not call any search-mcp Worker.
  • Your theme choice stays in your browser. If you pick light or dark, the choice is saved in this browser's local storage. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Hosting. Cloudflare serves the site. Like any web host, it receives each request, including your IP address and browser details, in order to answer it. Worker logs and analytics are off for this site.

The site's source is public, so you can check every claim above: skyphusion-labs/search-mcp-site.

The search-mcp software

search-mcp is software you run yourself, on your own Cloudflare account. Your corpus, your index and your tokens stay in that account. This website is documentation only.
